The Basics

What is AP?

Advanced Placement is a program run by College Board. Each AP course follows a university-level curriculum and ends with a standardized AP exam, written in May.

University-level courses

AP courses cover the material of an introductory university course, so you build the depth and study habits that first year will ask of you.

Open to every student

There's no application or admission process for AP courses at Georges Vanier. Choose them during regular course selection, like any other Grade 12 course.

Earn credit or placement

A strong exam score, usually a 4 or 5, can earn you credit or advanced standing at many universities in Canada and around the world. Policies vary by school and program.

The course and the exam are separate. An AP course earns you an Ontario credit, just like any other course. Writing the AP exam is an extra step that you register for yourself. See Exams & Registration below.

How to register

  1. Check that your exam is offered here

    We run the exams for the four AP courses taught at Georges Vanier. Exams for some other subjects may also be possible (see coinciding exams).

  2. Fill in the registration form

    Complete the AP exam registration form before the school's registration deadline.

  3. Join your exam section in My AP

    After you register, we'll send you instructions for joining your exam section in your College Board account.

  4. Pay the exam fee

    Pay the school by the registration deadline. That gives us time to order your exam from College Board.

  5. Write your exam in May

    Exams are held at Georges Vanier on College Board's dates. We'll confirm the room and what to bring closer to the day.

Coinciding exams

College Board sets one date and start time for each AP exam. When the exam for a subject we don't teach falls in the same time slot as an exam we're already supervising, we can sometimes seat you with that group.

AP Calculus AB Same date & time AP Calculus BC

For example, AP Calculus BC is often written at the same time as AP Calculus AB.

These spots depend on each year's College Board schedule, so they aren't guaranteed. You'd also be preparing on your own, since there's no GV class for that subject. Ask early.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to apply to take an AP course?

No. AP courses at Georges Vanier are open to every student. Pick them during regular course selection, the same way you choose any other course. Your guidance counsellor can help you plan the pre-AP courses in Grade 11 that lead into them.

Can I write an AP exam for a subject GV doesn't teach?

Sometimes. If College Board schedules that exam at the same date and time as one we're already supervising, we may be able to seat you with that group. AP Calculus BC alongside AP Calculus AB is the most common example. It depends on each year's schedule, so contact us early to ask.

How much does an AP exam cost?

The fee is set each year and is charged per exam. This year's fee will be posted here once it's confirmed.

When are AP exams written?

AP exams are held over the first two weeks of May, on dates and times set by College Board. See the College Board exam calendar ↗ for this year's schedule.

How are AP exams scored, and when do I get my results?

AP exams are scored from 1 to 5. Scores are released in July, and you'll see them in your College Board account. About AP scores ↗

Will universities give me credit for my AP exams?

Many universities in Canada and elsewhere award credit or advanced standing for strong AP scores, usually a 4 or 5. Every school and program sets its own rules, so check the universities you're interested in. Search university AP policies ↗

Your Grade 12 course mark counts toward your diploma and university applications like any other 4U course. The AP exam score is separate.

Can students from other schools write AP exams at Georges Vanier?

Yes. Students from other schools can register as exam-only candidates for the exams we administer. Exam-only registration has the same deadline as first-semester and full-year students. Contact us with any questions about which exams are available.
